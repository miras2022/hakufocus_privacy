1. Who we are

This policy covers the HakuFocus Android app (“app”), operated by BlueSample (“we”, “us”). HakuFocus helps you reduce distracting app use through app blocking, focus timers and short mindful rituals. Some entries you write (intentions, reflections) can be personal, so we treat everything in the app as private.

3. Device permissions

  • Usage access and installed-app list: to see which app is in the foreground and let you pick apps to block. This is core functionality, and the data is not shared with advertisers.
  • Display over other apps: to show the pause screen when you open a blocked app. We do not read what is on the screen.
  • Microphone, location, notifications: only for the features described in this policy. You can revoke them in Android settings.
  • Wi-Fi state: for Wi-Fi-based rules.
  • Foreground service, start at boot, battery optimisation exemption: to keep blocking reliable after a restart.
  • Internet access: for sync, sign-in, subscriptions, analytics, map tiles and optional alerts.

The app also keeps Phone, SMS, system settings and system UI out of blocking so you can always reach essential functions.

4. Witness and Telegram alerts (optional)

You may name an accountability contact (“Witness”). Their name and phone number are stored on your device, and synced to Firestore if you are signed in. Only add someone who agrees to this. HakuFocus does not send text messages itself.

If you set up your own Telegram bot and turn alerts on, the app sends a message directly from your device to Telegram when you bypass a block at a high friction level. The message includes your display name, the blocked app’s package name and the reason you gave. Your bot token and chat ID stay on your device. Telegram’s own privacy policy applies to that message.

5. Microphone and speech recognition

Breathing rituals analyse the microphone signal live on your device. Mantra rituals use Android’s speech recognition service, usually provided by Google. Depending on your device and whether an offline language pack is installed, your voice may be sent to that provider for transcription under its policy. HakuFocus receives only the recognised text, and we never store or receive recordings. A front-camera “mirror” preview may appear in some rituals; it is not recorded or transmitted.

6. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics) and Google Sign-In: Firebase privacy, Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services and Play Billing: geofencing, distribution and payments. We never see your card details.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions and receives your Firebase user ID and purchase data. RevenueCat privacy.
  • OpenStreetMap: the location picker downloads map tiles, so the tile servers can see your IP address and the area you view.
  • Telegram and your device’s speech service: only if you use those features.

These providers act under their own terms. We include no advertising SDKs.

7. How we use data

We use data to run the features you choose, sync your account, process subscriptions, keep the app reliable and improve it. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We disclose data only to the providers above, when the law requires it, or to protect rights and safety.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you clear it in the app’s Privacy Vault or uninstall. Cloud data stays while your account is active.

To delete your account and data:
  1. In the app, open Profile and choose Delete account. This removes your sign-in.
  2. Email bluesample0@gmail.com from your account address with the subject “Delete my HakuFocus data” to have your stored cloud records erased. We act within 30 days.

Purchase records held by Google and RevenueCat follow their own retention rules.

9. Security

Data travels to Firebase and RevenueCat over encrypted connections, app backups are disabled, and local data sits in the Android app sandbox. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete or restrict use of your data, and to object or withdraw consent. Email us to exercise them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Our legal bases are your consent (permissions, optional features), performing our service to you, and our legitimate interest in security and improvement.

11. Children

HakuFocus is not directed at children under 13, or under the higher minimum age in your country. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app for material changes. Questions or requests: bluesample0@gmail.com.
